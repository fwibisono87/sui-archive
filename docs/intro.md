# Welcome to the Suisei Archive Guide

Streams are temporary by nature. A VOD can disappear because it was intentionally unarchived, music rights changed, a channel closed, or a paid viewing window ended. This guide explains how to make a well-organized **personal copy of content you are allowed to access**.

The examples lean toward [Hoshimachi Suisei](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC5CwaMl1eIgY8h02uZw7u8A) and VTuber streams, but the workflow applies to many creators and platforms.

## Start here

If you have never used a command-line program, that is fine. Follow these chapters in order:

1. [Quick start](./quick-start.md) — make one complete YouTube archive.
2. [Prerequisites](./prerequisite-tools.md) — install and verify the tools.
3. [YouTube and yt-dlp](./tools/yt-dlp.md) — understand formats, cookies, and reusable options.
4. [Live streams](./live-streams.md) — prepare before a scheduled stream and recover afterward.
5. [Preservation](./preservation.md) — keep metadata, checksums, and backups alongside the video.

Paid events are covered separately in [Paid streams](./paid-streams.md). Platform behavior changes often, so those notes include a “last verified” date.

## Scope and boundaries

This guide is about preservation, not publication. Owning a ticket or channel membership does not normally give you permission to redistribute the recording.

:::warning Keep archives personal

- Do not claim a creator's work as your own.
- Do not republish members-only, ticketed, deleted, or unarchived material without permission.
- Follow the creator's wishes, the platform terms, and the law where you live.
- DRM-related downloading issues are outside this guide's scope. Please consult another resource if you encounter them.

:::

The tools described here can be used in many ways. You are responsible for confirming that your use is permitted.
